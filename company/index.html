How we work

Buying from a young company is a fair thing to be careful about.

So here is where we are, stated plainly rather than implied.

  • Funded. We are capitalised and not dependent on the next deal closing. We will walk your procurement team through the specifics under NDA.
  • A board by January 2027. Independent directors are being appointed now. Until then, governance sits with the three founders, and we are not going to dress that up.
  • Source code escrow on request. Small vendor risk is a real concern and it has a standard remedy.
  • Your artifacts are yours. Workflows, transforms and schemas export in open formats at any time. Nothing makes leaving expensive.
What we will not do
  • Claim a certification we do not hold
  • Ask you to send us PHI
  • Bill you per message and call it flexibility
  • Push a migration you do not need yet
