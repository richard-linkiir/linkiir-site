Personal Health Information — Data Protection Procedures

To the extent that any Personal Information, as defined below, is disclosed to, or comes into the possession of, Linkiir in any way, Linkiir shall abide by the information practices of the Subscriber and all applicable laws, including Privacy Laws, as defined below, and shall comply with the requirements of this Appendix.

A1. Personal Information

"Personal Information" means any information about an identifiable individual, or any information that is otherwise protected or regulated by any of the Privacy Laws, as defined below, that is provided by the Subscriber to Linkiir under the Agreement. For greater clarity, it includes personal information, as defined in the Personal Information Protection & Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and personal health information, as defined in the Personal Health Information Protection Act (Ontario) (PHIPA) and other applicable provincial health privacy statutes.

"Privacy Laws" (Canada)
All federal, provincial and local laws, rules, regulations, directives and governmental requirements governing the collection, use, disclosure, and security of Personal Information and applicable to any party to this Appendix, as same may be amended, interpreted, and supplemented by the courts and regulatory bodies from time to time. Privacy Laws include, without limitation and as applicable, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the Consumer Privacy Protection Act (CPPA), the Personal Health Information Protection Act (Ontario), and other applicable provincial health privacy statutes, and all other similar federal, provincial, and local requirements.
"Privacy Laws" (USA)
For USA-based Subscribers, "Privacy Laws" also means information as stated in the HITECH Act, 42 USC Section 17921; 45 CFR Subchapter A, Part 2; 45 CFR Section 164.501; 45 CFR sections 160.103 & 164.501; including the Privacy Rule found in HIPAA regulation 45 CFR Parts 160 & 164 Subparts.

A2. Authority to Process and Disclose

(a) Processing. Linkiir and its personnel shall process Personal Information only for the purpose of rendering services in accordance with the Order Form and as the Subscriber may otherwise instruct in writing.

(b) Disclosure. Linkiir and its personnel shall not disclose in any manner any Personal Information to any third party without the Subscriber's prior written consent.

A3. Personnel

(a) Access. Linkiir shall limit access to Personal Information to its personnel who have a need for access to the Personal Information for Linkiir to provide services under this Agreement.

(b) Training. Linkiir shall train personnel with access to Personal Information regarding the privacy, confidentiality and information-security requirements in this Appendix. Linkiir shall require personnel to agree, in writing, to comply with the terms of this Appendix. Linkiir shall exercise the necessary and appropriate supervision over personnel to maintain the privacy, confidentiality and security of Personal Information.

A4. Privacy and Security Obligations

(a) Compliance with laws. Linkiir and its personnel shall comply with: (i) all of the requirements of Privacy Laws currently in effect and as they become effective; (ii) all applicable industry standards concerning privacy, data protection, confidentiality or information security; and (iii) any applicable written instructions provided by the Subscriber relating in any way to the privacy, confidentiality and security of Personal Information, or applicable privacy policies, procedures, statements or notices that are provided to Linkiir in writing.

(b) Privacy and Security Program. Linkiir shall maintain a comprehensive written information privacy and security program that includes policies and procedures and risk management, monitoring, backup, disaster recovery and audit processes as necessary to comply with this Appendix and Privacy Laws. Linkiir's privacy and security program shall include appropriate and proportionate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards and other measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the Personal Information in its custody, in addition to its quantity, distribution, format and method of storage. Linkiir's privacy and security program shall be designed to: (i) ensure the security and confidentiality of Personal Information and safeguard it in accordance with Privacy Laws; (ii) protect against any anticipated threats or hazards to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Personal Information; and (iii) protect against any Privacy Breach.

(c) Information Segregation. Linkiir shall securely and physically segregate Subscriber Personal Information from any other Personal Information owned or managed by Linkiir or other third parties while providing services hereunder. Linkiir shall implement any necessary access barriers, password authorization procedures and other access controls and monitoring in connection therewith. Linkiir shall not retain or save any Subscriber Personal Information and, if required to process any of it, shall return (or destroy) the data once the service and support have been completed.

A5. Privacy Breaches

(a) Notification to Subscriber. Linkiir shall immediately, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, inform the Subscriber in writing of an actual Privacy Breach of which Linkiir becomes aware. Such notice shall summarize in reasonable detail the nature and scope of the Privacy Breach, the type(s) of Personal Information involved, the effect on the Subscriber of the Privacy Breach (if known), and the corrective action taken or to be taken by Linkiir.

(b) Approval of filings. The content of any filings, communications, notices, press releases or reports related to any Privacy Breach must be approved in writing by the Subscriber prior to any publication or communication thereof.

A6. Indemnification

Linkiir shall indemnify the Subscriber and its officers, employees, directors and agents from and against any Claims, to the extent that such Claims arise from, or may be in any way attributable to: (i) any violation of the procedures in this Appendix; (ii) the negligence, gross negligence, bad faith, or intentional or willful misconduct of Linkiir or its personnel in connection with the obligations set forth in this Appendix; (iii) Linkiir's use of any third-party contractor providing services in connection with or relating to Linkiir's performance under this Appendix; or (iv) any Privacy Breach involving Personal Information in Linkiir's possession, custody or control, or for which Linkiir is otherwise responsible.
