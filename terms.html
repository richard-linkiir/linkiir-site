1. Definitions

The following terms have the meanings set out below:

"Affiliate"
any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests.
"Agreement"
collectively, the signed Master Services Agreement, these Terms (where incorporated by reference), and each executed Order Form and Statement of Work.
"Client Data"
any data, information, or content (including Protected Health Information and Personal Information) that Client or its end users submit to, store in, or process through the Services.
"Deliverables"
any reports, documentation, configurations, scripts, channels, runbooks, or other tangible work product Linkiir creates specifically for Client under an SOW.
"Order Form" / "SOW"
a written ordering document referencing this Agreement that specifies the Services, fees, term, and (for SOWs) deliverables and milestones.
"Personal Information"
has the meaning given in the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (Canada) ("PIPEDA") and any applicable provincial statute, including the Personal Health Information Protection Act, 2004 (Ontario) ("PHIPA").
"Protected Health Information" / "PHI"
has the meaning given in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, as amended ("HIPAA").
"Services"
the support, managed services, implementation, migration, advisory, and (where licensed) Linkiir Engine subscription services provided by Linkiir under an Order Form or SOW.

The parties will execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) substantially in the form of Linkiir's standard BAA, which is incorporated by reference into this Agreement on execution. The BAA controls over conflicting provisions of these Terms with respect to PHI.

Linkiir's standard BAA addresses, at minimum: permitted uses and disclosures of PHI; safeguards reasonably designed to prevent unauthorized use or disclosure; reporting of security incidents and breaches of unsecured PHI; subcontractor flow-down; access, amendment, and accounting-of-disclosures support; return or destruction of PHI on termination; and government audit cooperation.

Linkiir may engage subcontractors that access PHI only where each such subcontractor has executed written terms imposing obligations no less protective than those in the BAA. A current list of approved subcontractors is available on request.

11. Data protection — PIPEDA & PHIPA (Canada)

To the extent Linkiir processes Personal Information subject to PIPEDA, or acts as an agent or service provider of a health information custodian under PHIPA, Linkiir will:

  • Process Personal Information solely on documented instructions from Client and only to the extent necessary to perform the Services ("minimum necessary");
  • Implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards proportionate to the sensitivity of the information, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and audit logging;
  • Where Linkiir is a PHIPA agent of a custodian, comply with the custodian's information practices, support patient access and "lockbox" directives, and not use or disclose PHI for any purpose other than performing the Services;
  • Notify Client without undue delay (and in no event later than 72 hours) of any actual or reasonably suspected breach of security safeguards involving Personal Information or PHI;
  • Cooperate with Client in responding to individual access, correction, and complaint requests under PIPEDA and PHIPA;
  • Where Personal Information is processed outside Canada, give Client reasonable prior notice of the country(ies) of processing and ensure comparable protection through contract.

Where required by Client or by law, the parties will execute a separate Data Processing Addendum reflecting these obligations and any additional provincial requirements.
