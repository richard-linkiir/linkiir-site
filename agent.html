Agent Capabilities

The messages you route
now do the work.

Agent reads every message your engine handles, flags what needs a decision, and proposes the fix. Approve, and it acts. Ignore, and it queues.

Detect

Duplicates,
drift, dead-letters.

Duplicate ADT detection, patient-match confidence, rejection clustering, quiet-endpoint alarms. Every anomaly worth reading is surfaced — the rest is filed.

Decide

Propose the fix,
not the ticket.

Agent explains what it saw and what it wants to do — merge, resubmit, re-route, hold. One-click approve. Or let it auto-resolve within your policy.

Resolve

Action Layer

A control surface
the interface team already trusts.

01 · Queues

One inbox for every interface

Rejections, unmatched patients, orphan orders, refill approvals — all in one review queue with owner, age and SLA visible.

02 · Policies

Auto-resolve within your rules

Define what Agent may do on its own and what it must escalate. Change the rules any day of the week — the audit log carries the diff.

03 · Handoffs

Slack, Teams, PagerDuty, email

When a human is needed, Agent pings the right one on the channel they already use — with the message body, the diff, and the approve button inline.
