How the engagement works

Practical support
without a tier-1 detour.

01 · Connect

Your team gets email and phone access to Linkiir integration engineers during the plan coverage window.

02 · Backstop

We help triage incidents, investigate break-fix work and support the integration engine and interfaces your team owns.

03 · Build

When included in your tier, we assist with design, build and testing for new interfaces alongside your team.

04 · Evolve

Documentation, migration guidance and HA architecture support keep the estate easier to operate as it grows.

In scope & out of scope

Support for
the integration layer.

( ✦ ) In scope

  • · Second-line incidents and break-fix for existing integrations
  • · HL7, X12, XML, REST and custom integration flows
  • · New integration design, build and test assistance where included by tier
  • · Interface inventory, message flows, runbooks and handover documentation
  • · Migration guidance for Rhapsody, Mirth, Cloverleaf, Corepoint and BizTalk estates
  • · High-availability architecture support for Professional and Enterprise plans

( ✦ ) Out of scope (by default)

  • · End-user help desk for clinicians, schedulers or front-office staff
  • · EHR application support outside the interface layer
  • · Network, firewall and infrastructure operations outside integration responsibilities
  • · Custom application development unrelated to integration
  • · Database administration unrelated to supported integration engines
  • · Hardware procurement and on-site break-fix

Additional scope can be added in an Order Form or handled as a separate engagement.
