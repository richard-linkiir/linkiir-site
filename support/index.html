How support works

Real engineers on the channel. Not a ticket queue.

Every Linkiir support engagement starts with onboarding: we inventory your channels, document your engine, plug into your monitoring and write the runbook with you. After cutover, your interfaces sit inside a service we actively operate — not a help desk that triages emails.

01

Monitoring

Channel-level health, queue depth, throughput, error rate and downstream acks — watched continuously with alerts tied to your SLOs, not generic platform noise.

  • Channel health — up/down, throughput, latency, ack lag
  • Queue depth — backlog detection with rate-of-change alerts
  • Dead-letter watch — bad-message capture and replay
  • Synthetic checks — round-trip probes on critical flows

02

Incident response

When an alert fires, a named engineer picks up the ticket, opens a war room, drives the fix and stays on the line until the channel is healthy — with you, not at you.

  • Named engineer — not a rotating outsourced contact center
  • War-room bridge — Teams, Slack, Zoom, or phone
  • Live status updates — every 30 min during P1
  • Containment first — restore traffic, then explain why

03

Root cause & prevention

Every P1 / P2 gets a written post-mortem within 5 business days, with a remediation list we own and report against. Repeat incidents are not acceptable.

  • Blameless RCA — timeline, contributing factors, fix
  • Remediation tracker — owned items, dates, status
  • Pattern detection — cross-incident trend reviews
  • Runbook updates — lessons fold back into ops docs

04

Change & release

Channel changes, code deploys, partner spec updates and engine upgrades — all handled through a Git-backed change process so a Tuesday afternoon doesn’t become a Tuesday night.

  • Change windows — planned, communicated, reversible
  • Git-backed channels — every change is reviewable
  • Pre-prod validation — full message replay where feasible
  • Rollback plan — documented before deploy, not after

05

Reporting & reviews

Monthly service reports your CIO can actually read, and a quarterly business review with your account lead — channel health, incident trends, capacity, roadmap items.

  • Monthly ops report — uptime, incidents, throughput
  • SLA scorecard — targets vs actual, with credits if missed
  • QBR — trends, risks, planned work, capacity outlook
  • Audit packs — on request, for HIPAA / SOC 2 reviewers

Incident lifecycle

What actually happens when something breaks.

  1. 01

    Detect

    Monitoring fires, or you raise a ticket by email. Severity is set in the first minutes based on documented criteria.

  2. 02

    Respond

    The on-duty engineer acknowledges within the SLA window, opens a bridge for P1 and starts investigation against the runbook. You get a real human, fast.

  3. 03

    Restore

    Containment and restoration first — reroute, replay, failover or rollback. We write the truth on the status update, even when the truth is awkward.

  4. 04

    Root cause

    Written post-mortem within 5 business days for P1, with remediation items we own, dates we commit to, and follow-up in the next monthly report.

In scope & out of scope

What Linkiir Support covers — and what it doesn’t.

In scope

  • HL7 v2, FHIR, X12, XML and custom channel support
  • Integration engine operations (Rhapsody, Mirth, Cloverleaf, Iguana, Corepoint, Linkiir Engine)
  • Channel monitoring, alerting and incident response
  • Trading partner and downstream system coordination
  • Code & config changes to in-scope channels
  • Engine upgrades, patching, version uplifts
  • Performance tuning and capacity planning
  • HIPAA / PIPEDA / PHIPA audit support and evidence packs

Out of scope (by default)

  • End-user help desk for clinicians, schedulers or front-office staff
  • EHR application support outside the interface layer
  • Network, firewall and infrastructure operations beyond integration hosts
  • Custom application development unrelated to integration
  • Database administration outside engine-owned databases
  • Hardware procurement and on-site break-fix

Most out-of-scope items can be added to a plan via Order Form or handled as a time-and-materials engagement. Ask us.
